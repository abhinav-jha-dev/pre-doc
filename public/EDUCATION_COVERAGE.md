# Anatomy education coverage

All 49 main structure groups have plain-language explanations of what they are and why they matter. All 13 systems have general care guidance and potential consequences of dysfunction, with linked health-information sources. Every displayed mesh resolves to these four sections.

Catalog audit:
- Male: 2,234 displayed mesh records; 1,792 map to a structure-group profile, 442 use a labeled system overview.
- Female: 1,212 displayed mesh records; 1,055 map to a structure-group profile, 157 use a labeled system overview.

A grouped explanation is not an independently reviewed description of every small mesh piece. The interface identifies this scope. Adapted female geometry remains reference material rather than verified individual anatomy. Further expert review is needed for the 599 system-level entries before claiming an individually authored atlas for every mesh.

Sources: MedlinePlus anatomy and system health topics, NIDDK digestive and kidney health, NHLBI heart health, and NIAMS bone/exercise guidance. Each profile links its care reference. This educational dataset is not a diagnosis or a personalized treatment plan.
